Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

This policy covers Tax Vault, a private desktop utility operated by its owner for personally invited users, and this public information site.

Information the desktop tool accesses

After an account holder grants access through Google OAuth, the tool can search Gmail and read selected message headers, message bodies, and attachments. It reads the mailbox email address to verify that credentials belong to the intended account.

The tool requests the Gmail gmail.modify permission because it both reads records and applies a label named vault-fetched to selected messages or threads. That Google permission is broader than reading alone. The tool’s implemented commands search, download, and label records; they do not send email.

Purpose and storage

Selected messages and attachments are downloaded to the operator’s local document vault for record organisation and review. OAuth credentials are stored in a separate local configuration directory. Those records and credentials are not uploaded to this public information site.

Sharing and connected services

Google processes authentication and Gmail API requests. The operator may separately choose to analyse selected downloaded records with an AI assistant or another service in their review workflow. Such processing is governed by that service’s terms and the operator’s settings and authorisation. The desktop download command itself does not send those records to this website.

Tax Vault does not offer advertising, sell Gmail data, or use Gmail data to build advertising profiles. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Downloaded records remain in the local vault until the operator deletes them, including any separately maintained copies or backups. Credentials remain locally until replaced or removed. An invited user can ask the operator to remove their locally stored credentials and records, subject to any record-retention obligations that apply to the operator.

Revoking access

You can withdraw Google access using your Google Account connections settings. Revocation stops future authorised access but does not automatically delete previously downloaded files. Contact the operator about those copies.

This public website

This site contains static information pages. It has no Gmail integration, contact form, advertising code, or application analytics code. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and operate the website.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the person who invited you to use Tax Vault. The app’s Google consent screen provides its configured support email. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
