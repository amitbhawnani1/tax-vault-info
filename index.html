A private desktop utility

Keep your records
in one place.

Tax Vault helps its owner and invited users organise selected email records into a local tax and investment document vault.

This is the tool’s public information site. It contains no financial records, account dashboard, Gmail sign-in, or document-upload form.

  1. 01

    Connect an authorised mailbox

    The desktop tool uses Google OAuth. Each invited user authorises their own Gmail account; the tool checks the account before saving its credentials.

  2. 02

    Collect selected records

    Search for relevant emails and download selected message text and attachments. A Gmail label marks records already fetched.

  3. 03

    Review in the local vault

    Downloaded records support the owner’s document organisation and review workflow. This public website cannot access those files.

Access and support

Tax Vault is for its owner and people they personally invite. It is not a public email service. For access, support, or a data request, contact the person who invited you to use the tool. The Google consent screen also identifies the configured support contact.

Read the privacy policy for details about Gmail permissions, storage, and revoking access, and the terms of use before using the tool.
