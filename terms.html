Terms of use

Effective 4 November 2026

Private, invited use

Tax Vault is a private desktop utility for its owner and personally invited users. This website describes the tool; it does not provide public registration, a hosted mailbox, or access to the operator’s records.

Authorised accounts and records

Connect only accounts you own or are authorised to use. Each account holder controls their Google consent. Use the tool only for records you are entitled to access and in accordance with applicable law, Google’s terms, and any relevant workplace rules.

Review the output

The tool helps organise documents. It is not professional tax, legal, or investment advice and does not submit tax returns or execute financial transactions. Check downloaded records and any subsequent summaries against the original source before making decisions.

Availability and account access

Operation depends on local software, Google services, and valid account authorisation. Access can stop if consent is revoked, credentials expire, or account policies change. No continuous availability or error-free processing is promised.

Privacy and ending use

The privacy policy explains data access and retention. You can stop using the tool and revoke its access in your Google Account settings. Contact the operator to discuss deletion of existing local copies.

Support

Contact the person who invited you to use Tax Vault. The configured support email is also available on the app’s Google consent screen.
